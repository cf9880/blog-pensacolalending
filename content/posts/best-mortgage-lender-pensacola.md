## How to choose a mortgage company in Pensacola: checklist

1. **Know your numbers.** Pull your credit and estimate a comfortable payment, including taxes and insurance.
2. **Get three quotes the same day** with the same loan amount, down payment, and lock period.
3. **Compare Loan Estimates** line by line, especially Section A (origination charges) and cash to close.
4. **Verify licenses** for the company and loan officer on [NMLS Consumer Access](https://www.nmlsconsumeraccess.org/).
5. **Get an insurance quote early.** In Northwest Florida, homeowners and flood insurance can change your budget.
6. **Ask your agent** which lenders close smoothly and communicate well.
7. **Get fully preapproved** (not just prequalified) before you make offers.

## Refinancing in Pensacola: what current homeowners should know

Freddie Mac's 30-year average was **7.32%** on Oct. 22, 2026, up from 6.19% a year earlier ([Freddie Mac PMMS](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms)). That means a rate-only refinance won't help many Pensacola homeowners right now. A refinance can still make sense if you want to:

- **Drop FHA mortgage insurance** by moving to a conventional loan once you have enough equity.
- **Take cash out** for storm repairs, a new roof, or to pay off high-interest debt.
- **Switch from an adjustable rate** to a fixed rate.
- **Use a HELOC** (home equity line of credit) instead, if you want to keep a low first-mortgage rate.

**Break-even point (definition):** closing costs divided by monthly savings. If you'll move before you break even, a refinance may not be worth it. Ask us for a free rate check and we'll compare your options honestly.
