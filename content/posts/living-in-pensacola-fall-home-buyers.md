**Late October and early November is one of the best times to see what living in Pensacola is really like. Foo Foo Festival runs October 29 to November 9, the Blue Angels Homecoming Air Show is November 6–7, and Halloween fills downtown. It's also a good time to tour homes with less competition.**

If you're thinking about moving here, or moving up within Escambia County, use the next two weeks to try out neighborhoods, test commute times, and get your financing ready.

## Key Takeaways

- **Foo Foo Festival:** October 29 – November 9, 2026, with arts, music, food, and culture events around the city.
- **Blue Angels Homecoming Air Show:** November 6–7 at NAS Pensacola. It's free, and adults need a REAL ID–compliant ID to enter the base.
- **Halloween weekend:** a Halloween Palafox Market on Saturday, October 31, plus haunted and theater events downtown.
- **Market snapshot:** Pensacola MLS single-family median of about $326,400 in September, about 40 days on market and 3.2 months of supply.
- **Rates:** 30-year fixed averaged 7.33% on October 26 (Mortgage News Daily).

## Which Neighborhoods Can You Check Out During Events?

Use event days to drive the area at busy times. Here's what to look for:

- **Downtown, East Hill, and North Hill:** Walk from Palafox Street to see historic homes and how close you are to restaurants, the waterfront, and festival venues. Check parking and noise on weekend nights.
- **Near NAS Pensacola and Warrington:** If you're military or work on base, air show weekend shows you real traffic at the gates. Our [VA loan guide for NAS families](/posts/va-loan-pensacola-nas-families/) covers financing.
- **Cordova Park and the Scenic Highway area:** These established neighborhoods are close to shopping, schools, and the bay.
- **Northern Escambia and Beulah:** Newer homes and more land, often with a longer drive downtown. Time the commute during rush hour.

Before you fall in love with a street, check the [FEMA flood map](https://msc.fema.gov/portal/home) and get an insurance quote. Hurricane season ends November 30, and insurance costs can change your budget as much as the rate.

1. **Get preapproved this week** so you can make an offer as soon as you find the right home.
