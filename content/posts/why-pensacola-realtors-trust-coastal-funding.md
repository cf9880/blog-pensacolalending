## What Do Local Agents Need From a Preapproval?

Listing agents in the Pensacola Association of REALTORS® market want to know the buyer can close, not just that someone typed a number on letterhead. A useful preapproval for a Pensacola offer usually reflects:

- A reviewed credit and income file, not a casual prequalification
- The loan program that actually fits (VA, FHA, conventional, or USDA)
- A realistic price range after insurance is considered
- The loan officer's direct phone number

It is still not a commitment to lend. Underwriting, the appraisal, and title work come next. But a letter that was issued after a real review helps your buyer compete, especially if another offer is cash or already underwritten.
