**The Pensacola housing market in fall 2026 is close to balanced.** The Pensacola Association of REALTORS® (PAR) reported a **$319,900 median sale price** for September 2026, slightly below a year earlier, while sales rose and pending contracts dipped. With 30-year mortgage rates near **7.4%**, buyers have more negotiating room than they've had in years, but payments are higher.

Below we break down the newest numbers, what Hurricane Isaias means for the market, and how to plan your move. We'll also share a few fun facts about the city we call home.

## Key takeaways

- **Median price:** $319,900 in September 2026 vs. $324,900 in September 2025 (PAR).
- **Sales up, pending down:** 843 closed sales (+5.7% year over year) but 235 pending sales (-8.9%).
- **Fewer homes for sale:** 3,114 single-family homes in inventory vs. 3,599 a year earlier.
- **Rates:** Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 7.40% on October 15, 2026.
- **Storm effect:** Hurricane Isaias hit on October 9, so October data may show delays.

## What is happening in the Pensacola housing market right now?

Here's the September 2026 snapshot from the [PAR market report](https://www.pensacolarealtors.org/reports), as summarized by the Emerald Coast Dispatch:

| Measure | September 2026 | September 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Total sales | 843 | 797 |
| Pending sales | 235 | 258 |
| Median sale price | $319,900 | $324,900 |
| New residential listings | 938 | 913 |
| Single-family inventory | 3,114 | 3,599 |
| Condo inventory | 440 | 461 |

A few other data points help round out the picture:

- **Realtor.com (October 2026, city of Pensacola):** median listing price $319,450, median 70 days on market, and median rent of $1,735 per month.
- **Momentum Realty's Market Pulse (Pensacola MLS, single-family, September 2026):** 3.3 months of supply, and 49% of active listings had cut their price as of October 20.

**What it means:** prices are roughly flat. Homes are still selling, but sellers are more willing to negotiate. That's good news if you're ready to buy.

## Where are mortgage rates in October 2026?

Rates rose sharply in September and early October, then eased a bit in the last week:

- **Freddie Mac PMMS:** the 30-year fixed averaged **7.40%** as of October 15, 2026, unchanged from the week before. A year earlier it was 6.27%. The 15-year averaged 6.71%. ([Freddie Mac](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms))
- **Mortgage News Daily:** the top-tier 30-year fixed index was **7.40%** on October 20, 2026, down from 7.43% the day before. The same index showed 30-year FHA at 7.02% and 30-year VA at 7.04%. ([Mortgage News Daily](https://www.mortgagenewsdaily.com/mortgage-rates))
- **Forecasts:** Fannie Mae's October 2026 forecast puts the 30-year near 7.4% in the fourth quarter and about 7.5% in 2027. The Mortgage Bankers Association expects about 7.3% for the fourth quarter.

**Mortgage rate (definition):** the yearly interest you pay on the loan balance. The **APR** adds certain loan costs, so it's a better way to compare offers.

Forecasts change all the time, and nobody can promise where rates will go. If waiting for a big drop doesn't fit your plans, a well-priced home today plus a refinance later (if rates fall and it makes sense) is a strategy many buyers consider.

## How did Hurricane Isaias affect the market?

Hurricane Isaias made landfall near Destin on October 9, 2026, as a Category 2 storm. Escambia County estimated about **$64 million** in public damage and recovery costs, not counting private property (WUWF). The September numbers above were recorded before the storm.

For buyers and sellers, expect:

- **Re-inspections and updated appraisals** on homes that were under contract.
- **Insurance questions:** carriers may ask for proof of no damage before they bind a new policy.
- **Some October closings to slip** by days or weeks.

## Fun facts: why people love living in Pensacola

- **America's first multi-year European settlement:** Tristán de Luna landed here in 1559, decades before St. Augustine. A hurricane in September 1559 wrecked much of his fleet, according to University of West Florida historians. (Pensacola knows storms, and it knows how to rebuild.)
- **The City of Five Flags:** Spain, France, Great Britain, the Confederacy, and the United States have all governed Pensacola.
- **The Cradle of Naval Aviation:** NAS Pensacola opened in 1914 as the nation's first permanent naval air station. The Blue Angels Homecoming Air Show is scheduled for **November 6–7, 2026**.
