**The Pensacola housing market is balanced heading into the holidays. In October 2026, the single-family median in the Pensacola MLS area was $324,900, flat from a year earlier. Homes took about 42 days to sell, supply sat near 3.1 months, and nearly half of active listings had cut their price.**

If you're thinking about buying or selling in Pensacola this winter, the latest numbers point to a steady market with room to negotiate. This November 2026 update covers Pensacola home prices, Escambia and Santa Rosa county trends, mortgage rates, and practical moves for buyers and sellers.

## Key Takeaways

- **Prices are flat:** The October single-family median was $324,900, 0.0% change from a year earlier.
- **Escambia vs. Santa Rosa:** Escambia's median was $299,900 (flat). Santa Rosa's was $345,000 (down 4.7%).
- **Balanced supply:** About 3.1 months of inventory, with 2,341 active listings as of Nov. 23.
- **Price cuts are common:** About 48% of active listings had reduced their price.
- **New homes matter:** New construction made up about 25% of single-family closings.
- **Rates are above 7%:** Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 7.28% on Nov. 19, 2026.

## What Are Pensacola Home Prices in November 2026?

The newest full month of data is October 2026. Here's how single-family homes performed across the Pensacola MLS area.

| Single-family, October 2026 | Closed sales | Median price | Change vs. Oct. 2025 | Median days on market |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pensacola MLS area (total) | 594 | $324,900 | 0.0% | 42 |
| Escambia County | 317 | $299,900 | 0.0% | 43 |

*Source: Momentum Realty, [October 2026 Pensacola Market Pulse](https://movewithmomentum.com/reports/pulse/pensacola/2026-10), computed from Pensacola Association of REALTORS® MLS records (data through Nov. 23, 2026).*

Two more details stand out:

- **Same-home resales still rose.** Momentum's "sold twice" measure, which compares the same house sold one to three years apart, showed about +3.4% per year. That suggests values held up even though the median was flat.
- **Condos are softer.** The condo median was $449,500, down 11.5% from a year earlier, with about 61 days on market.

## Is Pensacola a Buyer's or Seller's Market?

Pensacola is a **balanced** market right now. **Months of supply** measures how long it would take to sell every active listing at the current sales pace. Under about four months tends to favor sellers, and over about six tends to favor buyers. Pensacola's 3.1 months sits near the middle once you factor in price cuts.

Momentum Realty's Market Score, a 0-100 read of seller vs. buyer advantage, came in at 54, labeled "balanced, firm," up two points from September. About 14% of single-family homes sold over list price.

What that means on the ground:

- Well-priced, move-in-ready homes still sell in a few weeks.
- Homes that start too high often sit, then cut. That's where buyers find the best deals.
- With about 48% of listings showing a price cut, asking for seller-paid closing costs is reasonable in many cases.

## What Should Pensacola Buyers Do This Winter?

1. **Get preapproved before the holidays.** Fewer buyers shop between Thanksgiving and New Year's, and a ready buyer stands out.
2. **Compare loan types.** FHA and VA averages ran below conventional on Nov. 23. 3. **Look at new construction.** With about one in four sales being new homes, builder incentives may be worth comparing against resale deals.
4. **Ask for concessions.** Seller help with closing costs or a rate buydown can lower your cash to close or your payment.
5. **Enjoy the season while you shop.** Tour homes between Pensacola's downtown holiday festivities.

## What Should Pensacola Sellers Do?

- **Price to today's market,** not the 2022 peak. Escambia's median is about 7.4% below its peak 2022 month, and Santa Rosa's is about 8.0% below.
- **Prepare for winter showings** with good lighting, a tidy yard, and repairs finished up front.
- **Expect negotiation.** Offering to help with a buyer's closing costs can draw more offers than a small price cut.
