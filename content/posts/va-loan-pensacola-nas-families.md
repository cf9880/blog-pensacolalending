**A VA loan is often the best way for Pensacola military families to buy a home.** Eligible veterans and service members can buy with **$0 down**, pay **no monthly mortgage insurance**, and, with full entitlement, face **no VA loan limit**. With NAS Pensacola, Corry Station, and Saufley Field nearby, VA buyers are a big part of our local market.

Here's how the VA loan works in 2026, what it costs, and how to time a PCS move to Pensacola.

## Key takeaways

- **$0 down** for eligible borrowers, as long as the price doesn't exceed the appraised value.
- **No monthly PMI**, unlike most other low-down-payment loans.
- **2026 funding fee:** 2.15% for first use with under 5% down. Many disabled veterans are exempt.
- **No loan limit** with full entitlement, per the VA.
- **Rates:** Mortgage News Daily's 30-year VA index was 7.04% on October 20, 2026.

## What is a VA loan, and who qualifies?

A **VA loan** is a mortgage from a private lender that the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs partly guarantees. Because the VA backs part of the loan, lenders can offer $0 down and flexible terms.

You may qualify if you are:

- An active-duty service member who meets minimum service requirements
- A veteran with a qualifying discharge
- A National Guard or Reserve member who meets service requirements
- A qualifying surviving spouse

You'll need a **Certificate of Eligibility (COE)**. That's the VA document showing your entitlement. Your loan officer can usually pull it online in minutes.

**Entitlement (definition):** the amount the VA will guarantee on your behalf. With **full entitlement**, the VA says you have no loan limit, though the lender still checks your income, debts, credit, and the appraisal. ([VA.gov](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-limits/))

## How much does a VA loan cost in 2026?

The main extra cost is the **VA funding fee**, a one-time charge you can pay at closing or roll into the loan. Current rates for purchase loans, from [VA.gov](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/):

| Down payment | First use | After first use |
|---|---|---|
| Less than 5% | 2.15% | 3.3% |
| 5% or more | 1.5% | 1.5% |
| 10% or more | 1.25% | 1.25% |

**Example:** on a $300,000 VA loan with $0 down (first use), the funding fee is $6,450.

**Who's exempt?** Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability, certain surviving spouses, and active-duty members who've received a Purple Heart, among others. The VA says more than half of VA borrowers since 2021 were exempt.

Other costs are typical closing costs: appraisal, title, prepaid taxes, and insurance. In Florida, plan carefully for **homeowners insurance**, and ask about flood coverage too.

## How do VA rates compare right now?

On October 20, 2026, the [Mortgage News Daily](https://www.mortgagenewsdaily.com/mortgage-rates) index showed:

- 30-year VA: **7.04%**
- 30-year FHA: **7.02%**

VA rates often run below conventional rates. Your actual rate depends on your credit, the loan amount, and the lender, which is why shopping several lenders through a broker can help.

## Tips for a PCS move to NAS Pensacola

1. **Start before you get here.** Get preapproved with your orders in hand so you can shop online or with a local agent.
2. **Know your BAH.** Your housing allowance can count as income. Compare it to the estimated payment, including taxes and insurance.
3. **Think about the next move.** Many families keep their Pensacola home as a rental after their next PCS. VA loans require you to intend to live in the home at first, so ask how that rule works with future orders.
4. **Check commute routes.** Neighborhoods like Warrington, Perdido, and Southwest Escambia are close to the main gate. Families also look at Gulf Breeze and Pace.
5. **Ask about storm history.** After Hurricane Isaias, make sure inspections and the VA appraisal reflect any repairs. Our guide to [buying after Hurricane Isaias](../buying-home-pensacola-after-hurricane-isaias/) explains what to watch for.

**Already own with a VA loan?** The **VA IRRRL** (Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan) is a streamlined refinance with a 0.5% funding fee. It can make sense if rates drop meaningfully below your current rate.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I buy in Pensacola with no money down using a VA loan?**
Yes, if you're eligible and the price doesn't exceed the appraised value. Closing costs still apply, though sellers can sometimes help.

**Is there a VA loan limit in Escambia County for 2026?**
Not if you have full entitlement. The lender still decides what you can afford.

**How much is the VA funding fee in 2026?**
2.15% for first use with less than 5% down, 1.5% with 5% down, and 1.25% with 10% down. Many disabled veterans are exempt.

**Can I use a VA loan on PCS orders to NAS Pensacola?**
Yes. Lenders can often use your orders and intent to occupy to qualify you before you arrive.

**Do VA loans have mortgage insurance?**
No. There's no monthly PMI on a VA loan.
