**A conventional loan in Pensacola FL is a mortgage that isn't backed by a government agency. ## What Is the 2027 Conforming Loan Limit in Pensacola?

A **conforming loan** is a conventional mortgage that meets Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac rules, including a maximum loan size. FHFA sets that maximum each year based on national home prices, which rose 2.72% from the third quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026.

| Property type | 2026 limit | 2027 limit (Escambia and Santa Rosa) |
|---|---|---|
| One unit | $832,750 | $855,400 |
| Two units | $1,066,250 | $1,095,250 |
| Three units | $1,288,800 | $1,323,900 |
| Four units | $1,601,750 | $1,645,350 |

*Source: [FHFA 2027 conforming loan limits](https://www.fhfa.gov/data/conforming-loan-limit), county file published November 24, 2026, and FHFA 2026 county file.*

Loans above these amounts are usually **jumbo loans**, which often need larger down payments and more cash reserves. Most Pensacola buyers won't come close to the limit, but it matters for waterfront homes, Pensacola Beach purchases, and two- to four-unit properties where you live in one unit.

## How Does a Conventional Loan Work?

Conventional loans are the most common choice for buyers with steady income and solid credit. Here's what lenders usually look at:

- **Down payment:** as little as 3% on some programs for qualified buyers, often first-time buyers, per the Fannie Mae Eligibility Matrix. Many buyers put down 5% to 20%.
- **Credit score:** many lenders look for 620 or higher. Higher scores usually mean better pricing.
- **Debt-to-income ratio (DTI):** your monthly debts divided by your gross monthly income. Lower is better.
- **Occupancy:** conventional loans can finance a primary home, second home, or investment property, with different rules for each.
- **Seller help:** sellers can contribute toward your closing costs, within limits set by your down payment and loan type.

### What is PMI and when does it go away?

**Private mortgage insurance (PMI)** protects the lender when you put less than 20% down. Your cost depends on your credit score, down payment, and loan size.

Under the Homeowners Protection Act, you can ask your servicer to cancel PMI once your balance reaches 80% of the home's original value, if you meet the requirements. It ends automatically at 78% if you're current. The [CFPB explains the rules](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/when-can-i-remove-private-mortgage-insurance-pmi-from-my-loan-en-202/). That's a big difference from FHA, where annual mortgage insurance usually lasts for the life of the loan when you put less than 10% down, according to HUD's FHA handbook.

## Conventional vs. FHA: Which Is Better for Pensacola Buyers?

| Feature | Conventional | FHA |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum down payment | 3% on some programs | 3.5% with a 580+ score |
| Credit flexibility | Better pricing with higher scores | More flexible for lower scores |
| Mortgage insurance | PMI, removable at 78%-80% LTV | Annual MIP, often for the life of the loan with under 10% down |
| Property types | Primary, second home, investment | Primary residence only |

Many buyers with scores in the 700s and 5% or more down find conventional costs less over time. Buyers rebuilding credit may be better served by FHA. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the 2027 conforming loan limit in Pensacola?

FHFA set the 2027 one-unit conforming loan limit at $855,400 for both Escambia and Santa Rosa Counties, up from $832,750 in 2026. It applies to loans delivered to Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac starting January 1, 2027.

### How much do I need to put down on a conventional loan in Pensacola?

Qualified buyers can put as little as 3% down on some conventional programs, often for first-time buyers. With less than 20% down, you'll pay private mortgage insurance (PMI).

### What credit score do I need for a conventional loan?

Many lenders look for a score of 620 or higher. Higher scores usually earn better pricing and lower PMI. Requirements vary by lender and program.

### When does PMI go away on a conventional loan?

Under the federal Homeowners Protection Act, you can ask to cancel PMI when your balance reaches 80% of the home's original value, and it ends automatically at 78% if you're current on payments.

### Is a conventional loan better than FHA in Pensacola?

It depends on your credit and down payment. Conventional loans often cost less overall with good credit and let you drop PMI later, while FHA can be easier to qualify for with lower scores.
