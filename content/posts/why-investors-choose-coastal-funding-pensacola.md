## How Does a DSCR Loan Work on a Pensacola Rental?

**DSCR = monthly rent ÷ monthly PITIA** (principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and association dues).

A ratio of 1.0 means the rent covers the payment. Many lenders look for 1.0 or higher and offer better terms at higher ratios. Some allow a lower ratio with more down or a higher credit score.

In Pensacola, the "I" in PITIA deserves extra attention. Wind and homeowners insurance can be a large share of the monthly cost. A property that looks fine at a rough interest-only estimate can fall short once a real premium is included. Order an insurance quote while you are still comparing loans, and look at [wind-mitigation credits](/posts/pensacola-home-insurance-wind-mitigation/) if the house may qualify.

## What Other Financing Paths Can Investors Use?

Not every Pensacola investor uses DSCR.

- **Conventional investment loans** still look at personal income and tax returns. They can be a fit if your debt-to-income ratio has room and you prefer a qualified-mortgage structure.
- **Cash-out refinances or HELOCs** on a home or rental you already own can fund a down payment. Coastal Funding does offer HELOCs; see our [Pensacola HELOC guide](/posts/heloc-pensacola-fl/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use a DSCR loan to buy a rental in Pensacola?

Often, yes, if the property is an investment and the rental income supports the payment at a ratio the lender accepts. Coastal Funding offers DSCR loans in 40+ states, including Florida. Terms vary by lender.

### How does homeowners insurance affect a Pensacola DSCR loan?

Insurance is part of PITIA, the monthly payment used in the DSCR calculation. Higher Florida wind and homeowners premiums can lower the ratio, so get an insurance quote early before you rely on a target DSCR.

### What down payment do investors typically need in Pensacola?

Many DSCR purchases require about 20% to 25% down. Conventional investment loans also usually need more down than a primary-residence loan. Exact amounts vary by program and lender.

### Can I buy a Pensacola rental in an LLC?

Many DSCR programs allow the loan to be in an LLC, usually with a personal guarantee. Conventional investment loans have their own entity rules. Ask before you open or fund the entity.

### Does Coastal Funding finance short-term rentals in Pensacola?
