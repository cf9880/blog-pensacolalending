## What Does the Calendar Look Like From Mid-November?

Counted from today, November 15, 2026:

- **Thanksgiving, November 26:** about 11 days. That is shorter than a typical mortgage closing.
- **Christmas, December 25:** about 40 days. It can fit a 30- to 45-day contract, but it is tight once you subtract holiday office closures.
- **December 31:** about 46 days. That is a more comfortable window if documents, the appraisal, title work, and insurance stay on schedule.

[Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/learn/how-long-does-it-take-to-close-on-a-house/), citing a 2026 ICE study, puts the average close at about 37 days. That is a national figure, not a Pensacola promise. Local files can move faster when everyone is prepared, and they can move slower when a repair, a condo questionnaire, or an insurance binder lags.

If you already have an accepted contract, ask your loan officer whether the current date still works. If you are just starting to tour homes, treat a year-end move-in as possible, not automatic.

## Why Insurance Deserves a Head Start in Pensacola

In many parts of the country, homeowners insurance is a short checklist item. In Pensacola it can be a closing risk. Carriers may need a roof inspection, a wind-mitigation form, or extra time around the holidays. A binder that arrives late can stall the lender's clear-to-close.

Order quotes as soon as you are serious about a property, and read our guide to [Pensacola home insurance and wind mitigation](/posts/pensacola-home-insurance-wind-mitigation/). Build a closing date that is not the last open hour before Christmas.

You must also receive your [Closing Disclosure at least three business days before closing](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/when-do-i-get-a-closing-disclosure-en-179/). Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day do not count as business days for that waiting period.
