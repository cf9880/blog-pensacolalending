**Buying your first home in Pensacola is more doable than many renters think.** Qualified buyers can put as little as **3% down** with a conventional loan, **3.5% down** with FHA, or **$0 down** with VA or USDA. Escambia County and the Escambia County Housing Finance Authority also offer **down payment and closing cost help** for eligible buyers.

This guide covers loan options, local assistance, and the step-by-step path from renter to homeowner in the City of Five Flags.

## Key takeaways

- **Low down payments:** 3% conventional, 3.5% FHA, $0 VA and USDA for eligible buyers.
- **2026 limits:** $832,750 conforming and $541,287 FHA (one-unit, Escambia County).
- **Local help:** Escambia County's SHIP program and ECHFA's Classic DPA of up to $10,000.
- **"First-time" often means** no ownership of a primary home in the past three years.
- **Prices:** Pensacola's median sale price was $319,900 in September 2026 (PAR).

## What does a first-time buyer need to buy in Pensacola?

At a minimum, you'll need:

1. **Steady, documented income**, usually two years of work history (school or military service can count).
2. **A credit score that fits your loan.** FHA allows 3.5% down with a 580 score. Many conventional programs start around 620.
3. **A manageable debt-to-income ratio (DTI).** That's your monthly debts divided by your monthly gross income. Lower is better.
4. **Cash for down payment and closing costs**, unless a program, seller credit, or gift covers part of it.

**Closing costs (definition):** fees to finish the loan and transfer the home, such as appraisal, title insurance, and prepaid taxes and insurance. They often run a few percent of the price.

## Which loans work best for first-time buyers?

| Loan | Minimum down | Good fit for |
|---|---|---|
| Conventional | 3% (qualified first-time buyers) | Good credit; PMI can be removed later |
| FHA | 3.5% | Flexible credit; higher DTI |
| VA | $0 | Eligible veterans and service members |
| USDA | $0 | Eligible rural areas and income limits |

- **Conventional:** the 2026 conforming loan limit is **$832,750** for a one-unit home (FHFA). **PMI** (private mortgage insurance) is usually required under 20% down, but it can be removed once you reach enough equity.
- **FHA:** the 2026 Escambia County limit is **$541,287** (HUD). FHA has upfront and monthly mortgage insurance.
- **VA:** no monthly PMI and no loan limit with full entitlement. See our [VA loan Pensacola guide](../va-loan-pensacola-nas-families/).
- **USDA:** parts of Escambia and Santa Rosa counties outside the urban core may be eligible. Check an address on USDA's eligibility map.

## Is there down payment assistance in Escambia County?

Yes. Two local programs to know (always confirm current funding and rules):

**Escambia County Homebuyer Assistance Program (SHIP).** Run by the county's Neighborhood & Human Services Department, it offers down payment and closing cost help to income-eligible, mortgage-ready first-time buyers. Requirements include living in Escambia County, income at or below the area median, a prequalification or preapproval from a **SHIP-approved lender**, at least **$2,000** of your own toward closing, and a HUD-certified homebuyer education course. Funds are first-come, first-served. ([MyEscambia.com](https://myescambia.com/our-services/neighborhood-human-services/neighborhood-enterprise/homebuyer-assistance-program))

**Escambia County Housing Finance Authority (ECHFA).** ECHFA's Classic DPA offers up to **$10,000** as a 0%, 30-year deferred second mortgage. It isn't forgiven; it's repaid when you sell, refinance, or pay off the first mortgage. For Escambia and Santa Rosa, 2026 limits include household income up to **$102,694** (1–2 people) or **$118,099** (3+) in non-targeted areas, a **$566,354** purchase price cap (higher in targeted areas), and a **640** minimum credit score. Qualified veterans and buyers in targeted areas don't need to be first-time buyers. ([ECHFA](https://www.escambiahfa.com/participating-counties/escambia))

**Important:** these programs work only through approved or participating lenders. Ask us which programs can be paired with your loan before you apply.

## What are the steps to buy your first Pensacola home?

1. **Check your credit** and pay down high card balances if you can.
2. **Get preapproved.** You'll learn your price range and payment, and sellers take your offer seriously.
3. **Take a homebuyer class.** Many assistance programs require one, and it's useful anyway.
4. **Price insurance early.** Florida insurance can change what you can afford. Our [post-hurricane buying guide](../buying-home-pensacola-after-hurricane-isaias/) has tips.
5. **Shop with a local agent** and make an offer.
6. **Inspect, appraise, and close.** Many purchase loans close in about 30 to 45 days.

## Where do first-time buyers look in Pensacola?

Every budget is different, but first-time buyers often explore areas like Warrington, Brent, Ferry Pass, and Cantonment for value, or East Hill and North Hill for character near downtown. Realtor.com showed a **$319,450 median listing price** for the city of Pensacola in October 2026. See our [Pensacola housing market update](../pensacola-housing-market-fall-2026/) for more numbers.

## Fun facts for new Pensacola homeowners

- **Pensacola was settled in 1559** by Tristán de Luna, making it the first multi-year European settlement in what's now the U.S., according to University of West Florida historians.
- **It's the City of Five Flags:** Spain, France, Great Britain, the Confederacy, and the United States have all governed here.
- **Historic Pensacola**, run by the UWF Historic Trust, preserves museums and historic homes downtown. It's a fun first outing once the moving boxes are unpacked.
