## Key Takeaways

- **No down payment required** for eligible buyers and properties under USDA's guaranteed program.
- **Location matters.** Eligibility is set by USDA's map, address by address.
- **Income limits apply** to the whole household: $122,800 (1-4 people) and $162,100 (5-8 people) in Escambia and Santa Rosa counties for 2026.
- **Fees for FY 2027:** 1.00% upfront guarantee fee and 0.35% annual fee (USDA Rural Development).
- **Primary residences only.** No second homes or rentals.

## What Is a USDA Loan?

The **USDA Single Family Housing Guaranteed Loan Program** (often called Section 502 Guaranteed) lets approved lenders make home loans to low- and moderate-income buyers in eligible rural and suburban areas. USDA guarantees part of the loan, which is why lenders can offer financing with no down payment. You can read the program details on [USDA Rural Development's guaranteed loan page](https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/single-family-housing-guaranteed-loan-program).

A **guarantee fee** is USDA's version of mortgage insurance. There is a one-time upfront fee and an annual fee that is split into your monthly payment.

## Where Are USDA Loans Available Near Pensacola?

USDA decides eligibility by property address using its [eligibility map](https://eligibility.sc.egov.usda.gov/). In general:

- **Many addresses inside the city of Pensacola are not eligible**, because the program focuses on rural and smaller communities.
- **More rural parts of Escambia County**, especially north of the city, are where buyers most often find eligible homes.
- **Parts of Santa Rosa County** outside the more densely built areas may also qualify.

## What Are the USDA Income Limits in Escambia County for 2026?

USDA Rural Development published updated 2026 income limits on July 13, 2026. Escambia and Santa Rosa counties are listed at the standard Florida limit:

| Household size | 2026 income limit |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 4 people | $122,800 |
| 5 to 8 people | $162,100 |

The limit is based on **adjusted household income**, which counts everyone who will live in the home, not just the people on the loan. Certain deductions, such as for dependents and some childcare costs, can lower the amount tested. A separate rule is that you must qualify to repay the loan under the lender's guidelines.

## How Much Does a USDA Loan Cost?

USDA announced on September 16, 2026 that for fiscal year 2027 (October 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027):

- **Upfront guarantee fee: 1.00%** of the loan amount, which can usually be financed into the loan.
- **Annual fee: 0.35%**, paid in your monthly payment.

Interest rates on USDA loans are set by lenders, not USDA. For context, Mortgage News Daily's index put the 30-year FHA rate at 7.04% and the conventional 30-year at 7.46% on November 18, 2026, while Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 7.11% on November 12, 2026. Your own rate depends on your credit, the property and market conditions.

## How Long Does a USDA Loan Take to Close?

USDA loans need an extra step: after the lender approves the file, USDA Rural Development reviews it and issues a **conditional commitment**. That can add time compared with FHA or conventional loans.
