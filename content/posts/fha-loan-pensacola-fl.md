**An FHA loan in Pensacola lets eligible buyers purchase with 3.5% down and a credit score of 580 or higher. In 2026, the FHA limit for a one-unit home in Escambia County is $541,287, well above the area's median single-family price of about $308,000. You'll pay upfront and annual mortgage insurance.**

FHA loans are insured by the Federal Housing Administration, part of HUD. Because of that insurance, lenders can accept lower down payments and more flexible credit than many conventional loans. Here's what Pensacola buyers should know this fall.

## Key Takeaways

- **2026 Escambia County FHA limits:** $541,287 for one unit, $693,050 for two, $837,700 for three, and $1,041,125 for four.
- **Down payment:** 3.5% with a 580+ credit score; 10% with a score from 500 to 579.
- **Mortgage insurance:** 1.75% upfront, plus about 0.55% a year for most 30-year loans with less than 5% down.
- **Rates:** Mortgage News Daily's 30-year FHA average was 6.90% on October 30, 2026, below its 7.27% conventional average.
- **Local prices:** Escambia County's September 2026 median single-family price was $307,900.

Escambia County sits at the national FHA "floor," the lowest limit HUD sets. You can confirm any county with [HUD's FHA mortgage limits lookup](https://entp.hud.gov/idapp/html/hicostlook.cfm). For comparison, the 2026 conforming limit for a one-unit home is $832,750.

You can buy a two- to four-unit property with FHA if you live in one of the units. That can be a smart way to have rent help with the payment.

## What Do You Need to Qualify for an FHA Loan?

HUD sets the base rules, and lenders may add their own. In general you'll need:

- **A credit score of 580 or higher** for 3.5% down. Scores from 500 to 579 may qualify with 10% down.
- **Steady, documented income,** usually two years of work history.
- **A manageable debt-to-income ratio.** Lenders review your full monthly debts against your income.
- **A primary residence.** FHA loans are for homes you'll live in, not investment or vacation homes.
- **An FHA appraisal.** The home must meet HUD's minimum property standards for safety and soundness.

Your down payment can come from savings or an acceptable gift from family. Sellers may contribute up to 6% of the price toward your closing costs. See [HUD's buying page](https://www.hud.gov/buying/loans) for program details.

## How Much Does FHA Mortgage Insurance Cost?

FHA loans carry two mortgage insurance premiums:

- **Upfront MIP:** 1.75% of the base loan, usually added to the loan.
- **Annual MIP:** about 0.55% a year for most 30-year loans with less than 5% down, paid monthly. It's 0.50% with 5% to 10% down.

With less than 10% down, annual MIP generally lasts for the life of the loan. With 10% or more down, it ends after 11 years. Many buyers later refinance to a conventional loan once they have enough equity.
